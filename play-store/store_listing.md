## Full description (max 4000)
VangtiChai is a fast, simple change calculator for Bangladeshi Taka.

Type an amount on the large keypad and VangtiChai instantly shows the smallest number of notes that make it up: ৳500, ৳100, ৳50, ৳20, ৳10, ৳5, ৳2 and ৳1. There is no "calculate" button — the breakdown updates with every key you press.

Useful for shopkeepers, cashiers, delivery riders, students and anyone who counts cash.

FEATURES
• Instant note breakdown as you type
• A clear tile for every Taka note, highlighted when that note is needed
• Total number of notes shown at a glance
• Large keypad with Backspace (⌫) and CLEAR
• Works in portrait and landscape, and keeps your amount when you rotate
• Designed for both phones and tablets
• Works with a hardware keyboard
• Screen-reader (TalkBack) friendly labels
• Works completely offline

PRIVACY
• No ads
• No account or sign-in
• No internet access and no permissions
• No personal data is collected, stored or shared

HOW IT WORKS
VangtiChai always uses the largest note that fits, then moves to the next one. For Taka notes this always gives the fewest notes possible. Example: ৳688 = ৳500 + ৳100 + ৳50 + ৳20 + ৳10 + ৳5 + ৳2 + ৳1.

Amounts up to ৳999,999,999 are supported.
