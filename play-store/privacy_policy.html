Privacy Policy for VangtiChai

Effective date: 13 October 2026

VangtiChai ("the app") is a change calculator for Bangladeshi Taka, developed by Arafat Haque Biswas ("I", "me"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information the app collects

None. VangtiChai does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or non-personal information. The amount you type is kept only in your device's memory while the app is open, so it can be shown on screen and kept when you rotate the device. It is never saved to a file or sent anywhere.

Internet access and permissions

The app does not request internet access or any device permission (such as camera, location, contacts or storage). It works completely offline.

Third-party services

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and no sign-in or account system.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
