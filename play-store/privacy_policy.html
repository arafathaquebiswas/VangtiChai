Privacy Policy for VangtiChai

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: VangtiChai works completely offline. It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data, it requests no device permissions, and it contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

1. About this policy

This policy describes how the Android app VangtiChai (package name com.arafat.vangtichai), a change calculator for Bangladeshi Taka, handles information. The app is developed and published by Arafat Haque Biswas, an individual developer ("I", "me").

2. Information the app collects

None. VangtiChai does not collect, record, transmit, share or sell any personal or non-personal information, and it does not create user accounts.

The amount you type on the keypad is used only to calculate and show the note breakdown on your screen. Android may hold it temporarily so it is not lost when you rotate the screen or briefly switch to another app. It is never written to a file, database or settings by the app, never sent off your device, and it is discarded when you clear it or close the app.

3. Permissions and internet access

The app requests no device permissions — no access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files or phone. It does not request internet access and works fully offline.

4. Third-party services

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-media or tracking services, and no sign-in. It uses only Google's standard Android libraries (AndroidX), which do not collect personal data for the app.

5. Google Play

If you download VangtiChai from Google Play, Google may collect information as part of providing the Play Store (for example, download and installation data), as described in Google's Privacy Policy. I only see anonymous, aggregated statistics that Google provides to all developers, such as the number of installs; I do not receive your name or contact details from Google.

6. Data sharing and sale

Because the app collects no data, no data is shared with or sold to anyone.

7. Data retention and deletion

The app keeps no data about you, so there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app removes it and everything it contains from your device. If you email me, I use your message only to reply and delete it when it is no longer needed.

8. Security

Since no information leaves your device, no information about you is transmitted or stored on any server.

9. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no information from any user, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
